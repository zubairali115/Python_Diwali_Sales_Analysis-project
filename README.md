# Python_Diwali_Sales_Analysis-project
Python project for - Analyze Diwali sales data to improve customer experience and sales

## 📌 Project Description

This project analyzes **Diwali sales data** using Python to understand customer behavior, purchasing patterns, and business opportunities during the festive season. The analysis converts raw data into meaningful insights that can help businesses improve marketing strategies and increase sales.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main goals of this project were:

* Clean and preprocess raw sales data
* Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA)
* Identify high-value customers and top-selling products
* Generate business insights for festive marketing strategies

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **Python**
* **Pandas**
* **NumPy**
* **Matplotlib**
* **Seaborn**
* **Jupyter Notebook**

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset required preprocessing before analysis:

* Removed null values and duplicates
* Dropped irrelevant columns
* Converted data types for accurate analysis
* Ensured dataset consistency and reliability

Clean data helped produce accurate insights and visualizations.

---

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

### 👩 Gender Analysis

* Female customers contributed more to total purchases.
* Women showed stronger engagement in festive shopping.

**Insight:** Target female customers for festive marketing campaigns.

---

### 🎂 Age Group Analysis

* The **26–35 age group** had the highest purchasing activity.
* Represents young working professionals.

**Insight:** Focus marketing on young professionals.

---

### 🌍 State-wise Sales

Top performing states:

* Uttar Pradesh
* Maharashtra
* Karnataka

**Insight:** These states generate the highest festive revenue.

---

### 💼 Occupation Analysis

Top spending professions:

* IT Sector
* Healthcare
* Aviation

**Insight:** Working professionals are primary buyers during Diwali.

---

### 🛍️ Product Category Analysis

Most purchased categories:

* Food
* Clothing & Apparel
* Electronics

**Insight:** These products drive festive sales.

---

## 💡 Key Customer Segment

The ideal target customer profile:

**Married women aged 26–35 years from Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka working in IT, Healthcare, and Aviation sectors.**

They prefer purchasing:

* Food products
* Clothing
* Electronics

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

Businesses can increase festive sales by:

* Running targeted ads for young working women
* Focusing campaigns in high-performing states
* Offering festive bundles on top-selling products
* Providing special discounts for professionals

---

## 📈 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how **data analytics transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights**. Companies can use these insights to create targeted marketing campaigns and boost revenue during festive seasons.
